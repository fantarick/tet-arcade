# Code of conduct

TET Arcade is an experimental, open-source space for constructive technical work. Everyone participating in issues, pull requests, reviews, and discussions is expected to:

- Be respectful, inclusive, and patient with contributors of different backgrounds and experience levels.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Provide reproducible evidence and distinguish facts from hypotheses.
- Avoid harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, threats, and unsolicited promotion.
- Protect others' privacy and never publish credentials or identifying information without permission.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove abusive contributions and restrict participation when necessary. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer via their GitHub profile rather than posting sensitive reports in public issues.

We aim for a welcoming community where technical questions and good-faith disagreements are encouraged.
